The Journey

Three decades, told in moments.

1995

Lead DCS Commissioning Engineer

Where it began

Began his ABB journey leading commissioning projects for major power and water plants across Qatar and Cyprus. Successfully delivered complex control and automation systems for large-scale energy infrastructure.

1999

Head of Department – Sales & Execution

Building a new foundation

Played a key role in establishing ABB Power operations in Abu Dhabi, combining technical excellence with business leadership while guiding sales and execution across the region.

2008

Regional Operations Manager (HUB)

Leading the Middle East

Directed operations for ABB's Power Generation & Water business across the Middle East, managing large multidisciplinary teams, delivering major EPC projects, and driving operational excellence across the region.

2016

Regional Manager – Middle East Power & Water

Driving business growth

Took full profit and loss responsibility for the regional business, expanding ABB's digital portfolio while leading profitable operations, services, automation and electrical solutions across the Middle East.

2019

Director Operations – Energy Industries, IMEA

Executive leadership

Led operations across India, the Middle East and Africa for ABB's Energy Industries business, overseeing engineering, project management, supply chain, quality, digital services and strategic growth for one of ABB's largest global regions.

2023–2026

Regional Business Director & Cluster Manager

Special leadership assignments

Took on additional regional leadership responsibilities, launching new business initiatives across AMEA and successfully steering the Egypt Cluster through a challenging period while delivering exceptional business growth and organizational stability.

2026
